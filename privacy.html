Privacy Policy

Last updated: 6 September 2026

MG Bayan Lepas (Monthly Charges) is an internal tool used by Mintygreen staff at the Relau branch to record and manage resident monthly charges. This app is not available to the general public and is only used by authorized staff.

What information is collected

The app stores:

  • Staff login email addresses, used to sign in and identify who made changes
  • Resident names and room/reference numbers
  • Charge and billing records entered by staff

How this information is used

This information is used only to operate the billing system: recording charges, generating monthly totals, and keeping an audit history of changes made by staff. It is not sold, shared with advertisers, or used for any purpose outside running this internal system.

Where data is stored

Data is stored securely using Supabase, a third-party database provider. Access is restricted to authorized staff accounts.

Data retention

Records are retained for as long as needed for billing and audit purposes, in line with normal business record-keeping practice.
